Jessica Smith

Mental Health Nurse Practitioner

APRN, PMHNP-BC

Welcome to Smith Mental Wellness! My name is Jessica Smith, and I am a Psychiatric Mental Health Nurse Practitioner. My private practice is located in Kettering, Ohio, serving our local community and clients throughout the state. I am passionate about mental health care and enjoy providing a supportive, safe, and warm environment where individuals can find the care they need to achieve lasting mental wellness. I care for patients across the entire lifespan, starting with children as young as age 6.

I specialize in caring for those navigating PTSD, trauma, depression, anxiety, OCD, adjustment disorder, and Bipolar disorder. My clinical focus is on providing personalized, evidence-based medication management designed to meet each individual's unique needs, ensuring each client feels safe and heard every step of the way. This intentional approach to advanced psychiatric care is backed by over 14 years of clinical healthcare experience. This strong clinical foundation taught me that medication works best when it is tailored specifically to you—taking into account your lifestyle, environment, and medical history rather than just treating a list of symptoms.

To make care as accessible as possible, I offer flexible in-person appointments as well as convenient telehealth options. I welcome private pay and accept several major insurances, including Aetna, Anthem, Cigna, and Tricare, as well as others.

When I am not at the office, my absolute favorite place to be is surrounded by my family and finding new places to go hiking. If you are ready to partner together on your or your child's health journey, please reach out to schedule an initial consultation today.
